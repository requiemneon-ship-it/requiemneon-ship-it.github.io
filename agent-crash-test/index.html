What you actually get

1. Find

Loops, repeated side effects, state drift, identity drift, action-order errors, policy violations and wording-sensitive paths.

2. Shrink

Remove irrelevant turns until a smaller sequence still triggers the same failure.

3. Replay

Re-run the minimal reproducer several times instead of presenting a one-off anomaly as fact.

4. Export

Generate a regression artifact that preserves the rules required to detect the failure.

5. Fix

Your team patches the agent. We do not need production credentials.

6. Verify

Replay the same regression case and prove whether the defect still reproduces.
